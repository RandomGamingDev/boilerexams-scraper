This is a standard economic supply and demand graph. Below is an exhaustive description of its components:

### 1. Axes
*   **Vertical Axis (Y-axis):** Labeled **P** at the top. It represents price.
*   **Horizontal Axis (X-axis):** Labeled **Q** at the far right. It represents quantity.
*   **Origin:** The axes intersect at the bottom-left corner (0,0).

### 2. Plot Lines
*   **Supply Curve (S):** A straight line with a positive slope (bottom-left to top-right). It is labeled **S** near the top-right end.
*   **Demand Curve (D):** A straight line with a negative slope (top-left to bottom-right). It is labeled **D** near the bottom-right end.

### 3. Key Data Points and Intersections
*   **Equilibrium Point:** The Supply (S) and Demand (D) curves intersect at a single point.
    *   This point corresponds to **25** on the **Q** axis.
    *   This point corresponds to **75** on the **P** axis.
    *   A dashed line extends horizontally from this intersection point to the P-axis at the value 75.
    *   A dashed line extends vertically from this intersection point to the Q-axis at the value 25.

*   **Additional Point 1 (Supply Curve):**
    *   There is a dashed line extending horizontally from the Supply curve to the P-axis at the value **65**.
    *   This horizontal line intersects the Supply curve at a point where a vertical dashed line drops down to the Q-axis at the value **20**.

*   **Additional Point 2 (Demand Curve):**
    *   There is a dashed line extending horizontally from the Demand curve to the P-axis at the value **65**.
    *   This horizontal line intersects the Demand curve at a point where a vertical dashed line drops down to the Q-axis at the value **35**.

### 4. Summary of Coordinates (P, Q)
*   **Equilibrium:** (75, 25)
*   **Supply Curve Point:** (65, 20)
*   **Demand Curve Point:** (65, 35)
