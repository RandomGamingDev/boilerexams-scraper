This is a structural mechanics diagram depicting a system of two members interacting at a contact point.

### 1. Components and Nodes
*   **Member 1:** An angled bar pinned at point **A** to a vertical wall. The bar extends to point **B**.
*   **Member 2:** A horizontal bar pinned at point **D** to a vertical wall. The bar extends horizontally and interacts with the angled bar.
*   **Point A:** Pin support at the lower left wall.
*   **Point B:** The end of the angled member. A vertical cable/rod is attached here, extending upward to a fixed ceiling support.
*   **Point C:** A contact point located on the underside of the horizontal member. Member 1 (specifically the top surface at/near B) supports Member 2 from below at point **C**.
*   **Weight W:** A block resting on the horizontal member between points C and D.
*   **Point D:** Pin support at the upper right wall.
