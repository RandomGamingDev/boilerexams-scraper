This flowchart depicts an experimental design process, progressing from left to right through a series of blocks, treatments, and data collection points.

### 1. Structural Overview
The diagram is oriented horizontally. It starts from a single node on the left, branches into three parallel paths, and converges into a single node on the right. Purple arrows represent the direction of flow.

### 2. Node Descriptions (Text and Visual Style)

**Leftmost Node:**
*   **Text:** "Experimental Units"
*   **Shape:** Rounded blue rectangle.

**Middle "Block" Layer:**
*   Three parallel nodes, stacked vertically.
*   **Appearance:** Rounded blue rectangles labeled "Block 1", "Block 2", and "Block 3" from top to bottom.
*   **Connections:** One purple arrow points from the "Experimental Units" node to each of the three "Block" nodes.

**"Treatment" Layer:**
*   Three parallel nodes, stacked vertically.
*   **Appearance:** Rounded white rectangles with black borders.
*   **Labels:** "Treatment 1" (top), "Treatment 2" (middle), "Treatment3" (bottom).
*   **Connections:** One purple arrow points from each "Block X" node to its corresponding "Treatment X" node.

**"Record Response" Layer:**
*   Three parallel nodes, stacked vertically.
*   **Appearance:** Rounded red rectangles.
*   **Labels:** "Record response" (identical text in all three).
*   **Connections:** One purple arrow points from each "Treatment X" node to its corresponding "Record response" node.

**Rightmost Node:**
*   **Text:** "Compare Results"
*   **Shape:** Yellow scroll icon.
*   **Connections:** One purple arrow points from each of the three "Record response" nodes to this central "Compare Results" node.

### 3. Spatial Relationships
*   The diagram is organized into four distinct vertical stages/columns:
    1.  Experimental Units
    2.  Blocks 1–3
    3.  Treatments 1–3
    4.  Record Response 1–3
*   The final stage, "Compare Results," acts as a terminal convergence point for all three data paths.
*   The layout is symmetrical relative to the middle horizontal axis (passing through Block 2).
