*   $X_C < X_B < X_A$.
*   $Y_A < Y_C < Y_B$.
*   The budget lines ($M_1$, $M_2$, and the dashed line) share the same X-intercept $T$ or are parallel in the case of the dashed line, but the diagram specifically shows the dashed line passing through point $C$ as a parallel shift to the budget lines.
