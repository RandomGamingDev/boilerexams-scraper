This image is a standard MOSFET output characteristic graph (Drain Current $I_D$ versus Drain-Source Voltage $V_D$).

    *   Point 2: $(2, 2.0)$

*   The grid consists of vertical lines at $V_D$ integer intervals and light horizontal lines at increments of 0.5 mA (though the major labels are at 2, 4, 6, 8).
