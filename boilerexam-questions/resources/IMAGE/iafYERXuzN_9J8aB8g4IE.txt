This is a circuit diagram of a common-source amplifier configuration featuring an N-channel enhancement-mode MOSFET.

### **Circuit Components and Layout**
1.  **MOSFET:**
    *   Located in the center of the diagram.
    *   It is an N-channel MOSFET (enhancement mode).
    *   The **Gate** is connected to the positive terminal of the input voltage source.
    *   The **Source** is connected directly to ground.
    *   The **Drain** is connected to the output node and to resistor $R_D$.

2.  **Input Voltage Source:**
    *   Located on the left side.
    *   It is a DC voltage source with a time-varying component, labeled as $V_G + v_g(t) \text{ V}$.
    *   The positive terminal is oriented upward, and the negative terminal is connected to ground.

3.  **Output Section:**
    *   An output node is present at the drain of the MOSFET.
    *   $V_D$ is measured across the output, with the positive terminal at the drain and the negative terminal at ground.

4.  **Drain Circuit:**
    *   Resistor $R_D = 2000 \text{ } \Omega$ is placed in series between the drain and the supply voltage.
    *   The supply voltage $V_{dd} = 24 \text{ V}$ is connected to the other side of $R_D$.
    *   Current $I_D$ flows downward from the supply through $R_D$ into the drain of the MOSFET.
