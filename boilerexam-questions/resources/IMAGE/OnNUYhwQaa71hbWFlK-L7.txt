This is a structural diagram of a simply supported beam subject to a linearly varying distributed load.

### 1. Geometry and Layout
*   **Beam:** A horizontal rectangular beam of length $L$.
*   **Supports:**
    *   **Support A (Left):** A pin support located at $x = 0$.
    *   **Support B (Right):** A roller support located at $x = L$.
*   **Loading:** A triangular distributed load acts downwards on the top surface of the beam. The intensity of the load is zero at $x = 0$ and increases linearly to a maximum value of $q_0$ at $x = L$. 
*   **Visual Representation of Load:** Six downward-pointing arrows are depicted along the beam, increasing in length linearly from left to right to represent the distribution.

### 2. Labels and Variables
*   **Coordinates:** The horizontal axis along the beam is defined by $x=0$ at the left support (A) and $x=L$ at the right support (B).
*   **Load Intensity:** The variable $q_0$ is placed at the top right of the triangle, indicating the maximum magnitude of the distributed load.
*   **Node Labels:** 
    *   "A" is centered below the left pin support.
    *   "B" is centered below the right roller support.

### 3. Summary of Key Elements
*   **Left Boundary ($x=0$):** Pin support, load intensity = 0.
*   **Right Boundary ($x=L$):** Roller support, load intensity = $q_0$.
*   **Load Type:** Linearly distributed (triangular profile).
*   **Coordinate System:** 1D horizontal axis $x$, where $0 \le x \le L$.
