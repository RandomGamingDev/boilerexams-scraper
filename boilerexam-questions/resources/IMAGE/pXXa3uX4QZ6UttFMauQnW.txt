This circuit diagram is a single-loop-like structure (with an open output terminal) featuring an independent voltage source, two resistors, and a dependent voltage source.

*   **Output:** The rightmost section of the circuit is open, with a vertical arrow labeled $I_N$ indicating a current path shorting the output terminals (implicitly defining a Norton current setup).
