This circuit diagram depicts an AC circuit featuring an ideal transformer, a current source, and several impedances.

### 1. Circuit Topology
The circuit consists of two loops coupled by an ideal transformer.

**Primary Loop (Left):**
*   Contains a current source of $j10\text{ A}$ pointing upwards.
*   Connected in series with a resistor of $10\ \Omega$.
*   Connected to the primary winding of the transformer.
*   A voltage $\tilde{V}_1$ is defined across the primary winding, with the '+' terminal at the top and '-' at the bottom.
*   A blue dot is positioned at the top terminal of the primary winding.

**Secondary Loop (Right):**
*   Connected to the secondary winding of the transformer.
*   Contains an impedance of $-j4\ \Omega$ in series with a resistor of $3\ \Omega$.
*   A voltage $\tilde{V}_2$ is defined across the secondary winding, with the '+' terminal at the top and '-' at the bottom.
*   A blue dot is positioned at the top terminal of the secondary winding.
*   An arrow indicates the current flow direction for $\tilde{I}_2$ through the top branch (pointing to the right).
